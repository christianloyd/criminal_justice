How the Pillars Interconnect

Crime Occurs

Reported to barangay tanod, PNP, NBI, or other agency. Initial response and crime scene investigation begins.

Arrest & Investigation

Law enforcement pillar conducts investigation, gathers evidence, and apprehends suspects. Inquest or PI follows.

Prosecution & Trial

Judicial pillar takes over. Prosecutor files Information; court conducts arraignment, trial, and renders judgment.

Corrections

Convicted persons serve sentences in BuCor/BJMP facilities. Rehabilitation programs + parole/probation by PPA.
