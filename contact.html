About This Resource

This website was developed as an educational resource on the Philippine Criminal Justice System. It is intended for students, researchers, and anyone seeking to understand Philippine criminal law and procedure.

About

Purpose & Scope

This interactive website covers the five pillars of the Philippine Criminal Justice System — from its historical foundations and constitutional framework, through its structure, criminal laws, law enforcement agencies, courts, and corrections pillar, and its ongoing challenges.

It was built to transform static reference material into an engaging, accessible, and visually consistent educational experience suitable for academic use, professional orientation, and public legal education.

Important Disclaimer
This website is for educational purposes only. It does not constitute legal advice. Information about laws and procedures is general and may not reflect the most recent legislative amendments. Consult a licensed Philippine attorney for specific legal guidance.
