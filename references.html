References & Readings

Foundational texts and contemporary research grounding the theories discussed on this site.

On Crimes and Punishments (1764)

Cesare Beccaria

The foundational text of the Classical School, arguing against torture and for proportional deterrence.

The Criminal Man (1876)

Cesare Lombroso

The text that birthed the Positivist school, introducing the concept of the atavistic "born criminal."

Principles of Criminology (1939)

Edwin H. Sutherland

Introduced Differential Association Theory, establishing that criminal behavior is learned through social interaction.

Causes of Delinquency (1969)

Travis Hirschi

The defining text for Social Control Theory, outlining the four social bonds: Attachment, Commitment, Involvement, Belief.

Crime and Everyday Life (1994)

Marcus Felson

Expands on Routine Activity Theory, demonstrating how the physical environment and daily routines structure crime opportunities.
