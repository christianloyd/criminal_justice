Reform Efforts

Key Reform Initiatives

Continuous Trial Rule

SC AM 15-06-10-SC: strict time limits on hearings and decisions, reducing indefinite postponements.

Recognizance Act

RA 10389: allows indigent accused to be released without monetary bail — decongesting jails.

DNA Act

RA 10867: expanded use of DNA evidence and post-conviction DNA testing via NBI.

Good Conduct Law

RA 10592: Good Conduct Time Allowance incentivizes program participation and reduces sentences.
